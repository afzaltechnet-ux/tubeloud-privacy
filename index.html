Privacy Policy – TubeLoud (YouTube Audio Enhancer)

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

TubeLoud – YouTube Audio Enhancer ("TubeLoud", "we", "our") is a free browser extension that boosts the volume of YouTube videos up to 400%. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what the extension accesses, and what it does not do with your data.

2. Short Version: We Collect Nothing

TubeLoud does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data of any kind. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • No browsing history or tab URLs
  • No audio content or recordings
  • No cookies, analytics, or tracking
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs

3. How TubeLoud Works (100% Local Processing)

TubeLoud adjusts video volume using your browser's built-in Web Audio API. All audio processing happens locally on your device, in real time. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent to any server — because there is no server.

4. Permissions We Use & Why

  • YouTube tab access: To detect the video player and apply volume controls on the current tab only.
  • Local storage: To remember your preferences (volume level and the "Auto-apply" toggle) on your device. This data never leaves your browser and can be removed by uninstalling the extension.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share.

6. Children's Privacy

TubeLoud does not knowingly collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
